About Mazao Fresh Kenya

Who We Are

Mazao Fresh Kenya is a youth-led agribusiness start-up headquartered in Nakuru Town, Nakuru County. We were founded to bridge the gap between smallholder farmers growing crops like tomatoes, kales (sukuma wiki), avocados, and maize, and buyers in major urban centers such as Nairobi, Nakuru, and Eldoret[cite: 2].

Currently, many rural farmers sell their produce through middle brokers at unfairly low prices, while urban buyers struggle to source fresh produce at affordable rates[cite: 2]. Mazao Fresh Kenya is taking this agribusiness online to build a direct, transparent supply chain that benefits both farmers and consumers[cite: 2].

Our Mission

"To empower smallholder farmers across Kenya by providing an accessible digital marketplace that guarantees fair pricing, reduces post-harvest loss, and delivers fresh, high-quality agricultural produce directly to urban households and businesses."

Our Strategic Objectives

To fulfill our mission, we are focused on achieving the following key milestones:

  1. Eliminate exploitative broker chains to ensure farmers earn fair prices for their produce[cite: 2].
  2. Build an easy-to-use online catalogue connecting rural farmers to urban buyers[cite: 1, 2].
  3. Integrate secure M-Pesa mobile payment solutions for seamless digital transactions[cite: 2].
  4. Provide automated SMS notifications to notify farmers instantly when orders are placed[cite: 2].
  5. Deliver real-time weather forecasts via Weather API updates to help farmers plan harvest times[cite: 2].
