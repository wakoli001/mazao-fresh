Welcome to Mazao Fresh Kenya

Mazao Fresh Kenya is a youth-led agribusiness start-up based in Nakuru Town, Nakuru County. Our mission is to eliminate unfair broker prices by connecting smallholder farmers directly to household and restaurant buyers across urban markets including Nairobi, Nakuru, and Eldoret.

Through our digital platform, farmers get fair compensation for their hard work while buyers enjoy fresh, high-quality farm produce delivered straight to their doorstep at affordable prices.

What We Offer

We source a wide variety of fresh agricultural products directly from local farms in Nakuru County:

  • Vegetables: Fresh tomatoes, kales (sukuma wiki), spinach, onions, and carrots.
  • Fruits: Ripe avocados, passionate fruits, and fresh bananas.
  • Cereals: Quality maize, beans, and dry grains.
  • Dairy: Fresh farm milk and local dairy products.

Planned Digital Services

We are continuously upgrading our technology to better serve our farming community and customers:

  • Online Produce Catalogue: Browse fresh produce directly from your phone or computer.
  • M-Pesa Integration: Fast, safe, and convenient mobile money payments for all orders.
  • SMS Order Alerts: Real-time SMS notifications sent to farmers for new customer orders.
  • Weather API Updates: Providing accurate weather forecasts to help smallholder farmers plan harvests.
